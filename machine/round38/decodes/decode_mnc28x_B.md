Notice from the みどりが丘三丁目町内会 (Midorigaoka 3-chome Neighborhood Association): participants wanted for the year-end night watch (歳末夜警 / 火の用心パトロール).

This year too, for year-end fire prevention, we will hold a patrol from December 26 to December 30, for 5 days.

The meeting place is in front of the 三丁目集会所 (3-chome community hall). Group 1 gathers at 19:30 and disbands at 20:30. Group 2 gathers at 21:00 and disbands at 22:00. On December 30 only, there is no Group 2; only Group 1 will be held.

The association will provide wooden clappers (拍子木) and flashlights. Reflective vests will be provided only to those who applied by December 20.

Applications must be made by the participant themselves, either by filling in the form on the circular board (回覧板) or by phoning the office (事務局). The exception is elementary school students, whose applications are made by their guardian.

Elementary school students may join Group 1 only, and only if accompanied by a guardian. Junior high school students may join Group 1 only.

Participants will receive a 500円 shopping-street gift voucher per session. Those who join on all 5 days will receive an additional 20% of their total amount.

Vouchers will be handed only to the participant themselves, after disbanding on December 30, at the 集会所 (community hall). Collection by proxy is not allowed.

After the patrol, hot 豚汁 (pork miso soup) will be served at the 集会所. It is free for participants; family members who did not participate pay 150円 per bowl.

For lost items and consultations about changing groups, contact the office: 田村恵子, 090-4417-2286, during hours of 10:00–18:00.

If you will be absent on the day itself, tell your group leader directly: for Group 1, 森川; for Group 2, 大野.

The patrol is held even on rainy days. It is cancelled on days when a strong-wind advisory (強風注意報) is in effect; this will be announced on the association bulletin board by 17:00.
