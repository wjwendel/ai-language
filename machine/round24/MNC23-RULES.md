## R12 Threshold scope repeated in the over-limit clause
- `may <verb> <object> yourself up to N; <object> above N: need <who>'s approval` — the object is restated in the over-limit clause (never a bare `above N:`).

## R8b Never compute what the original doesn't state (amends R8)
- If the original gives a rule ("75% of the course fee") and the base sits elsewhere, keep the rule as written next to the base; do not write the derived amount unless the original states it.

## R13 Section-level conventions instead of repeated words
- An instruction section header ends with `you`; inside it, `may` lines mean "you may do this yourself" — drop `yourself` on may: lines (keep it on call lines: `call N yourself`, R7).
- Drop the generic party word (`customer`) where the line has only one possible party; keep it where two parties appear on the line.
- Group repeated `never: contact or copy` objects into one clause: `never contact or copy: X about A; Y about B`.
- Street addresses: keep only if a question could plausibly need them (keep name + city/street once, drop house-number-only redundancy is NOT allowed: numbers are facts — keep full address, but drop the descriptor words around it).
- All safety rules unchanged: may: lines, who-is-who line before naming rules, prohibitions folded into the action, thresholds stated as a decision, no safety rule in parentheses or after "|", R7 number next to "call", R9 identity as written, R10 indefinite actors.
