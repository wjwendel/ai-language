## §4 → English

Lindenau city library will close from February 3rd for approximately four months due to renovation. Library head Katrin Vogel announced that they will merge three reading rooms and set up twelve new computer stations. The renovation costs around €2.4 million, with the state paying two-thirds of the cost. During the closure, readers can borrow books from a temporary branch in the town hall, which will be open from Tuesday to Friday from 10 AM to 5 PM. Borrowed media do not need to be returned, and loan periods will be automatically extended until the end of June. There will be no late fees during this time, but magazines cannot be borrowed from the temporary branch because there is no space. Vogel stated that the library had over 80,000 visitors last year. The reopening is planned for early June, but it could be delayed if materials are delivered late.
