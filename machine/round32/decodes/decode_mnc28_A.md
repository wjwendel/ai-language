The Sakuradai 3-chome Neighborhood Association will hold a disaster-preparedness drill (organized by the neighborhood association) on November 8.

Participants assemble at 9:30 a.m. at Sakuradai Second Park. The drill starts at 10:00 and is scheduled to end at 11:45. Participation is free.

On the day, emergency drinking water will be distributed to all households, 2 bottles per household, along with the disaster-preparedness helmets that were ordered by October 25. Each helmet costs 1,800 yen; the neighborhood association subsidizes one third, and each household pays the remaining amount. Payment is cash only, at the reception desk on the day.

All participants must bring their own flashlight; the neighborhood association will not lend any.

The fire extinguisher hands-on session is limited to junior high school students and older.

In case of rain, the drill will be held in the Second Elementary School gymnasium. If a heavy rain warning is in effect, the drill will be cancelled and postponed to November 15 at the same time.

Exception: people aged 70 or older and households with infants or young children are considered to have participated, even without coming to the park, by displaying a safety confirmation card at their front door. Safety confirmation cards will be distributed from November 1 to November 5, 13:00–17:00, at the neighborhood association meeting hall.

Stretcher-carrying practice is only for the group leaders designated by the disaster-preparedness officer.

Notices of absence and questions should be made by phone by November 6 to the disaster-preparedness officer, Kenji Morita (090-4417-2385). The deputy officer, Misaki Takahashi, is in charge of reception on the day and does not accept phone contact.

After the drill, at 12:00, there will be a soup kitchen serving pork miso soup (tonjiru) at the meeting hall, one bowl per person. Please bring your own tableware.
