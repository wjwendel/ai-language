Notice from みどりが丘三丁目町内会: participants wanted for the year-end night watch (歳末夜警 / 火の用心パトロール).

This year too, we will patrol for year-end fire prevention. The patrol runs for 5 days, from December 26 to December 30. The meeting place is in front of the 三丁目集会所. Group 1 gathers at 19:30 and disbands at 20:30. Group 2 gathers at 21:00 and disbands at 22:00. On December 30 only, there is no Group 2, only Group 1.

The association provides wooden clappers (拍子木) and flashlights. Reflective vests are provided only to those who applied by December 20.

Participants must apply themselves, either by filling in the form on the circular board (回覧板) or by phoning the office (事務局). The exception is elementary school children, whose applications are made by a guardian. Elementary school children may join Group 1 only, and only if a guardian accompanies them. Junior high school students may join Group 1 only.

Participants receive a 500円 shopping-street gift voucher for each session. Those who join all 5 days receive an additional 20% of the total amount. Vouchers are given only to the participants themselves, after disbanding on December 30, at the 集会所. They cannot be collected by a proxy.

After the patrol, hot 豚汁 is served at the 集会所. It is free for participants. Family members who did not take part pay 150円 per bowl.

For lost items and requests to change groups, contact the office: 田村恵子, 090-4417-2286, hours 10:00–18:00. If you will be absent on the day itself, tell your group leader directly: 森川 for Group 1, 大野 for Group 2.

The patrol goes ahead even on rainy days. It is cancelled on days with a strong-wind advisory (強風注意報). Any cancellation will be announced on the association bulletin board by 17:00.
