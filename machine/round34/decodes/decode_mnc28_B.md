Notice of Renovation of Songquan Bathhouse, Donghu Subdistrict

To all residents:

The bathhouse will be closed for renovation from December 1 to December 20, and will reopen at 6:00 a.m. on December 21.

Multi-visit ticket books, regardless of purchase date, and monthly passes purchased before November 15 may be extended by the same number of days as the closure. Monthly passes purchased on or after November 15 cannot be extended, but a refund may be applied for, at 60% of the face value. Each multi-visit ticket book contains 10 tickets and sells for 150 yuan; each monthly pass costs 280 yuan.

Applications for refunds or extensions must all be submitted before December 10. They must be handled in person by the ticket holder, bringing their ID card, on the first floor of the Subdistrict Service Center. The only exception is that for elderly people over 70, a family member may handle it on their behalf by bringing a photocopy of the elderly person's ID card. Applications by telephone or online are not accepted. The Service Center's hours are Monday to Friday, 9:00–16:30, with a break from 12:00 to 13:00.

Personal belongings in the bathhouse storage lockers must be removed before 20:00 on November 30. Items not collected by then will be registered by staff and kept until December 31, after which they will be treated as unclaimed property. The locker key deposit is 20 yuan per key, fully refunded when the key is returned.

After the renovation, new shower equipment will be installed in both the men's and women's bathing areas, and an accessible changing room will be added. During the first three days after reopening, December 21 to December 23, residents over 65 may enter free of charge once by presenting their ID card.

For comments or inquiries during the renovation, please contact the person in charge, Zhao Liping, at 0512-6688 3417, on working days from 8:30 to 17:00.
