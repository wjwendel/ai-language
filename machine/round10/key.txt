MNC13 key. One fact per line. §N xx = section N, source language xx; render only in the requested language. "task xx; you:" = orders to the reader; under that header the subject "you" is omitted. "X: Y" = when/if X, do Y. "may: A" = A allowed; "never: A" or "never A" = A forbidden. "without B" = B forbidden while doing the action. "Name = Role" = identity fact. call X "Y", never "Z" = refer to X as Y, not Z. "more than N" = strictly above N. @HH or HH:MM = 24h time; Mar3 = date. + = and. → = goes to/moves to. Source-language words are exact terms; keep them.
