MNC9 key. One fact per line. §N xx = section N, source language xx; render only in the requested language. "task xx; you:" = orders to the reader; under that header the subject "you" is omitted. "if X:" then indented lines = X applies to every indented line. "may: A" = A allowed; "may: A | never: B" = A allowed, B forbidden; "never: A" = A forbidden. "Name = Role" = identity fact (own line). "call X \"Y\" | never \"Z\"" = refer to X as Y, not Z. @HHMM or HH:MM = 24h time; Dec5 = date. > < strict; ≤ ≥ inclusive; ">N (N no)" = threshold strictly above N. + = and. → = goes to/becomes. Source-language words are exact terms; keep them.
