§4
On October 6, the city of Freiburg announced that from March 1, 2027, a packaging tax (Verpackungssteuer) will apply to disposable tableware in the food service industry.

The tax will be €0.50 for each disposable cup and bowl and €0.20 for disposable cutlery, capped at €1.50 per meal.

Expected revenue is about €1.8 million per year, all of which will go to street cleaning (Stadtreinigung).

Businesses with fewer than 3 employees will pay only half in the first year.

Environment mayor Lena Krüger said: „Wer Mehrweg anbietet, spart bares Geld.“

The hospitality association Dehoga said it will have the bylaw judicially reviewed by the end of January.

An information event for businesses will take place on November 12 at 18:00 at the town hall (Rathaus).
