This is the spring lending and return notice for the 햇살마을 씨앗도서관 (seed library), on the 1st floor of the 햇살마을 주민센터. The service starts this spring.

The library is open from April 5 to April 26, every Saturday from 10:00 to 13:00, in the front yard of the community center. On rainy days it moves to the 2nd-floor auditorium.

Each person may borrow up to 3 packets of seeds and up to 2 pots of seedlings, and may borrow both together. The seedlings are tomato, chili pepper and eggplant. Each variety is limited and given out first come, first served.

A volunteer writes each loan in the ledger, so borrowers do not need to write anything. Seed loans are free. Seedlings carry a deposit of 2,000 won per pot, and you get half of the deposit back when you return the empty pot.

Loans are only for residents whose address is in 햇살마을, and you must bring ID that shows your address.

By April 19, please return all native bean seeds you borrowed and any lettuce seeds borrowed before March 10. Return seeds you harvested yourself, in the same amount you borrowed.

You must return seeds in person. Nobody may return them on your behalf, except that family members may return seeds for residents aged 70 or over. Anyone with unreturned seeds cannot borrow new seeds until they return them.

If you want to donate or exchange seeds, tell the info desk during open hours. Only self-harvested seeds can be donated, not store-bought ones. You may exchange up to 2 packets at a time.

On April 12 at 11:00 there is a free class, '베란다 텃밭 가꾸기' (balcony vegetable garden), in the 2nd-floor auditorium. It has 20 places, and you must apply by phone by April 9 at 18:00. Participants receive 1 packet of lettuce seeds as a gift, which does not count toward the loan limit.

Check the harvest year and germination rate printed on the envelope, and ask the volunteers if you have questions.

Parking is tight, so please walk or bike if you can.

For questions, contact the seed library manager 한서윤 at 010-4471-2286 (weekdays 9:00–17:00).
