MNC15 key. One fact per line. §N xx = section N, source language xx. "task xx; you:" = orders to the reader; subject "you" omitted. "X: Y" = if/when X, do Y. "may: A" = A allowed; "never A" = A forbidden; "without B" = B forbidden while doing the action. "role: Name, not Other" = identity. call X "Y", never "Z" = how to name X. "more than N" = strictly above N. + = and; → = moves to. Source-language words are exact terms.
