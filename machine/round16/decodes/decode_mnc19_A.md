On November 10, the Shenzhen transport bureau announced that from January 1, 2027, all new taxis must be pure electric. The city currently has about 21,000 taxis, 62% of which are pure electric.

The government will pay drivers a subsidy of ¥30,000 per taxi for the early scrapping of fuel-powered taxis; drivers can apply until December 31, 2026. In addition, 120 new fast-charging stations will be in service before June next year, with a full charge taking about 35 minutes.

The bureau's director, 刘建华, said: "这是全国力度最大的出租车电动化计划之一。"

The taxi drivers' association, however, is worried about the drop in driving range in winter and is demanding that the subsidy be raised to ¥50,000.
