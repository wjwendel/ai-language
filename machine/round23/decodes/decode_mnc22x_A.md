This is the 桜台町内会 autumn festival notice. The festival will be held on October 12 (Saturday) and October 13 (Sunday) at 桜台第二公園.

On both days the festival runs from 10:00 to 20:00. The exception is the 盆踊り on October 13, which runs from 18:30 to 20:30, extended by 30 minutes.

Admission is ¥500 for adults and ¥200 for elementary school pupils; preschoolers are free. For households that have not paid this year's association fee, admission is ¥800 per adult.

Stall applicants must bring their application form and the ¥3,000 stall fee to the reception desk at the 町内会館 by September 20.

We are asking for help with setup on October 11 (Friday) from 16:00, and with cleanup on October 14 (Monday, a public holiday) from 9:00.

In case of rain, the festival will be postponed to October 19 (Saturday); this will be announced at 7:00 that morning over the neighborhood broadcast. If it also rains on the 19th, the festival will be cancelled and stall fees will be refunded in full.

There is no parking, so please do not come by car. The exception is wheelchair users, for whom 5 temporary parking spaces are available on the north side of the park.

Contact: festival committee chair 森田和夫, 090-4417-2286.
