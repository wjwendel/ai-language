Monthly vendor-invoice check. On the first business day of each month at 10:00 a.m. Eastern, open the Vendors folder in Drive and the Payables sheet. For each new invoice, check the amount against the purchase order and add a row to the Payables sheet. Never pay an invoice yourself; only Grace Liu in accounting can release payments. Invoices over $5,000 need written approval from Daniel Brooks, the operations director, before Grace pays them. You may fix a misspelled vendor name in the sheet, but don't edit invoice amounts or due dates. If an invoice has no purchase order number, email the vendor once asking for it, and don't add a row until they reply. Call Grace the payables lead, never the controller; the controller is Helen Park, and do not copy Helen on vendor emails. If the Payables sheet is open by someone else, wait 15 minutes and try again, at most twice. Don't reply to vendors who threaten late fees; forward those messages to Grace without answering. Finish by 3 p.m., and every Friday at noon send Daniel a summary of invoices waiting for approval.
