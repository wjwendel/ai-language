§1
Hilltop Café's weekly supplier orders: each Thursday at 2 p.m. Central, check the Stock sheet and Gmail Suppliers inbox, and draft orders (using the "Weekly Order" template) for items below reorder level. Rosa, the café manager, must approve every order via the approval card; orders over $600 also need review by Daniel in accounting first, though he isn't copied on supplier emails. Quantities may be adjusted up to 10% to meet supplier minimums, but products and brands can't be switched. Price-increase notices go to Rosa unanswered. Never contact owner Kevin Brandt about orders, and don't order by phone. If the Stock sheet hasn't been updated since Monday, stop and alert Rosa. Drafts are due by 4 p.m. Thursday; on the month's last Thursday, send Rosa the monthly order total.

A piscina municipal coberta de Vila Nova da Serra fecha de 2 a 30 de março para substituir o aquecimento, numa obra de cerca de 410 mil euros (75% fundos europeus), segundo a vereadora Ana Lopes. As aulas de natação infantis passam para a piscina de Ribeirinha, com transporte gratuito às terças e quintas após a escola; março não é cobrado e maiores de 65 anos podem usar o ginásio do pavilhão sem custo. A obra de 2019 acabou duas semanas antes do previsto. Reabre a 31 de março com entrada livre.
