§1
Weekly volunteer-shift routine for Eastside Food Pantry: Tuesdays at 7:30 p.m. Central. Check Shifts sheet, Volunteers inbox. For new sign-ups, verify background checks; send missing checks to Marcus Bell. Shifts with fewer than 3 volunteers need a reminder post by Priya Shah. Swap volunteers with written agreement. Volunteers under 16 can only take Saturday shifts with a parent. Call Marcus, not Ana Torres, the director. If over 40 sign-ups for a Saturday, inform Marcus. Do not reply to rule complaints; forward them to Marcus. Finish by 9 p.m. Send Ana monthly volunteer hours on last Tuesday.
§2
Valdecastro cortará el puente de San Telmo del 9 al 27 de febrero para reparar juntas de dilatación. Cuesta 310.000 euros, Diputación paga 70%. Autobuses línea 4 desviados, parada en calle Cervantes. Peatones y bicicletas usarán pasarela provisional. Vecinos de la Ribera pueden aparcar gratis con tarjeta de residente. Ambulancias tendrán paso libre.

From Mar 16, weekly Thursday market in Borgo San Michele moves to communal stadium parking lot for 4 months while piazza Garibaldi is repaved. Cost: 780,000 euros, half funded by Region. Bins reduced to 60, assigned by lottery Mar 2. Free shuttle every 20 min from 7-13 at station. Commune cuts occupation tax by 50% until July. Dogs allowed on leash only.
