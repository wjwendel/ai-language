The library will be open from April 5 to April 26, every Saturday from 10:00 to 13:00, in the front yard of the community center. On rainy days it will be held in the 2nd-floor auditorium instead.

Each person may borrow up to 3 packets of seeds and up to 2 pots of seedlings, and you may borrow seeds and seedlings together. The seedlings on offer are tomato, chili pepper and eggplant. Quantities of each variety are limited and go on a first-come, first-served basis.

A volunteer will write your loan record in the ledger, so you don't need to write it yourself. Seed loans are free. Seedlings carry a deposit of 2,000 won per pot, and you get half the deposit back when you return the empty pot.

Loans are only for residents whose address is in 햇살마을, and you must bring ID that shows your address.

By April 19, please return all native bean seeds you borrowed and any lettuce seeds you borrowed before March 10. Return seeds you harvested yourself, in the same amount you borrowed. You must return them in person, and proxy returns are not accepted. The exception is residents aged 70 or older, whose family may return seeds for them. Anyone with unreturned seeds cannot take out new seed loans until those seeds are returned.

If you'd like to donate or exchange seeds, tell the information desk during open hours. Only seeds you harvested yourself can be donated, not store-bought seeds. You can exchange up to 2 packets at a time.

On April 12 at 11:00 there will be a free class, '베란다 텃밭 가꾸기' (Balcony Vegetable Gardening), in the 2nd-floor auditorium. There are 20 places, and you must apply by phone by 18:00 on April 9. Each participant gets one packet of lettuce seeds as a gift, which does not count toward the loan limit.

Please check the harvest year and germination rate on the envelope, and feel free to ask the volunteers.

Parking is limited, so please walk or bike if you can.

Contact: 한서윤, seed library manager, 010-4471-2286 (weekdays 9:00–17:00).
