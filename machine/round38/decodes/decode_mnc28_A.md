Every estimate must be sent within 2 working days after the radio arrives, and general questions should be answered within 1 working day. Customers should send the original back panel with the radio, but this is not required. Every customer must include their order number in the subject line; if it is missing, ask for it before replying to anything else.

Collectors' Club members get free return shipping on orders above $250; for everyone else, standard return shipping costs $22.

On Saturdays, do not confirm any drop-off appointment; offer only weekday slots instead.

Midorigaoka 3-chome Neighborhood Association: Call for participants in the year-end night watch (the "beware of fire" patrol). As every year, this is for fire prevention at the end of the year.

The night-watch patrol will run for five days, from December 26 to December 30. The meeting place is in front of the 3-chome community hall. Group 1 assembles at 19:30 and disbands at 20:30; Group 2 assembles at 21:00 and disbands at 22:00. However, on December 30 only, Group 2 will not be held, and the patrol will be done by Group 1 alone.

The neighborhood association will provide wooden clappers (hyoshigi), flashlights and reflective vests, but only for those who apply by December 20.

Applications must be made by the participants themselves, either by filling in the application form on the circular board or by telephoning the office. The exception is elementary school children, whose applications are made by a parent or guardian. Elementary school children may take part in Group 1 only if accompanied by a parent or guardian; junior high school students may take part only in Group 1.

Participants receive 500 yen worth of shopping-street vouchers per session; those who take part on all five days receive an additional 20% of their total. The vouchers will be handed only to the participants themselves, at the community hall after disbanding on December 30; they cannot be collected by a proxy.

After the patrol, warm tonjiru (pork miso soup) will be served at the community hall: free for participants, and 150 yen per bowl for family members who are not participants.

For lost property and for requests to change groups, contact the office: Keiko Tamura, 090-4417-2286 (hours 10:00 to 18:00). To report an absence on the day itself, contact your group leader directly (Group 1: Morikawa; Group 2: Ōno).

The patrol will go ahead even on rainy days. It will be cancelled on days when a strong wind advisory is in effect, and this will be announced on the neighborhood association's bulletin board by 17:00.

If a horse owner asks for a postponement, you may postpone their appointment once, by up to 3 days.

Cancellations received less than 24 hours before the appointment incur a 45 € cancellation fee, except when the horse is sick and a veterinary certificate exists. Cancellations by phone count only once they have additionally been confirmed by email.

Customers must state the equine passport (Equidenpass) number when booking. They should send X-rays if the horse is getting orthopedic shoes, but this is not mandatory.

On Wednesdays, the farriers drive only to district South (Landkreis Süd); requests from district North (Landkreis Nord) get no appointment on Wednesdays.

New customers get their first appointment at the earliest 7 days after the request; regular customers can get one after just 2 days.

Emergency: if a horse owner reports that the horse is suddenly severely lame after shoeing or has stepped on a nail, call the veterinary emergency service at 0171 448 2093 yourself immediately. Then tell the customer two things: first, that the veterinarian will contact them within 30 minutes; second, that they must keep the horse in the stall themselves and must not pull out the nail. Never ask the customer to call the emergency service themselves.

Every Monday at 7:30, send the weekly schedule to Konrad Pichler. On the last Friday of every month at 15:00, send the material report to Johanna Kerschbaumer.

If a supplier announces a price change, forward the email to Johanna Kerschbaumer; do not answer it yourself. Forward complaints about a farrier's work to Selin Aydemir; complaints about invoices go to Johanna Kerschbaumer.
