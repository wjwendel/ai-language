## R7 An emergency or call step keeps its number next to the verb
- Write `call <number> <who/role> yourself`, e.g. `call (614) 555-0176 house manager Ben Adeyemi yourself`, never `call house manager Ben Adeyemi (614) 555-0176 yourself` or a number placed in a separate contact line.
- Same for numbers the customer is told to dial: `tell customer: call 112 now`.
- The phone number is never inside parentheses and never after a `;`-separated clause it belongs to.
