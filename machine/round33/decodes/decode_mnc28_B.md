[Haeden-gu Public Health Center] Notice of Free Autumn Health Checkups

Free health checkups for district residents will run from November 3 to November 21. Checkup hours are weekdays from 8:30 to 11:30 a.m.; on Saturday, November 15 only, there will be special checkups from 9:00 a.m. to 12:00 noon. The checkups take place in the examination room on the 2nd floor of the health center; registration is at the civil service counter on the 1st floor.

The checkups are for Haeden-gu residents aged 40 or older (international age). When you visit, you must bring your ID and the checkup notice you received by mail after October 1. If you have lost the notice, it can be reissued at the registration counter, and there is no fee for reissuing it.

If you are having a blood test, you must fast from 9 p.m. on the day before the checkup; water is allowed. You must mark whether you have fasted on the questionnaire yourself; staff will not check or mark it for you.

All basic checkup items are free. Optional items are a bone density test at 15,000 won and a thyroid ultrasound at 40,000 won; the cost of optional items is paid at registration, in cash or by card. Residents aged 65 or older pay only 50% of the cost of optional items. However, basic livelihood security recipients receive the optional items completely free as well, regardless of age.

Results will be mailed within 14 days of the checkup date; if you would like to be notified of your results by text message, request this when you register. For schedule changes or questions about results, contact Officer Park Seon-young of the health center's Health Promotion Team at 031-770-4128.

Cancellations on the day of the checkup can be made by phone only; cancellations up to the day before can also be made on the health center's website.

During the checkup period, the health center parking lot is free for up to 2 hours, and 1,000 won is charged for every 30 minutes after that. For the 3 days before your checkup, avoid heavy drinking and strenuous exercise. You may bring up to 1 companion; if you come with an infant or young child, please use the waiting area in front of the examination room. Flu vaccinations will not be given during this checkup period. If you have limited mobility, a wheelchair will be prepared if you contact us in advance.
