Home delivery is free within 5 miles. For more than 5 miles, it costs $7. For more than 15 miles, delivery is not offered.

Never tell anyone except the patient or a listed caregiver which medications the patient takes. Never cc the owner, Gary Feld, on patient messages.

Every Wednesday by 4pm, send Dr. Brooks a list of prescriptions that have been waiting more than 3 days for pickup.

§4
On October 6, the city of Freiburg announced that from March 1, 2027, a packaging tax (Verpackungssteuer) will apply to disposable tableware in gastronomy. The tax is €0.50 for each disposable cup and bowl and €0.20 for disposable cutlery, capped at €1.50 per meal. Expected revenue is about €1.8 million per year, all of which will go to street cleaning (Stadtreinigung). Businesses with fewer than 3 employees will pay only half in the first year. Environment mayor Lena Krüger said: „Wer Mehrweg anbietet, spart bares Geld.“ The hospitality association Dehoga says it will have the bylaw judicially reviewed by the end of January. An information event for businesses will be held on November 12 at 18:00 at the town hall (Rathaus).
