Haetsal Village Seed Library: Spring Lending and Return Guide

The Seed Library is on the first floor of the Haetsal Village Community Center, and it opens for the first time this spring. It runs from April 5 to April 26, every Saturday from 10:00 to 13:00, in the front yard of the Community Center. On rainy days it moves from the front yard to the auditorium on the 2nd floor of the Community Center.

Each person may borrow up to 3 packets of seeds and up to 2 pots of seedlings. You can borrow seeds and seedlings together. The seedlings available are tomato, chili pepper and eggplant. Quantities of each variety are limited and given out on a first-come, first-served basis.

A volunteer records each loan in the ledger, so you do not need to fill in a record yourself. Borrowing seeds is free. Seedling pots carry a deposit of 2,000 won per pot, and half of the deposit is returned when you bring back the empty pot.

Only residents whose address is in Haetsal Village may borrow, and you must bring an ID showing your address.

The return deadline is April 19. By then, return all native bean seeds you borrowed, plus any lettuce seeds borrowed before March 10. Return them with seeds you harvested yourself, in the same amount you borrowed. The person who borrowed must return the seeds in person, and no one else may return them on your behalf. The exception is seniors aged 70 or older, whose family members may return seeds for them. Anyone with unreturned seeds cannot borrow new seeds until their return is complete.

To donate or exchange seeds, speak to the information desk during opening hours. Donations must be seeds you collected yourself, not seeds bought in a store. Exchanges are limited to 2 packets at a time.

On April 12 at 11:00 there is a free class, '베란다 텃밭 가꾸기' (Balcony Vegetable Gardening), in the 2nd-floor auditorium. It holds 20 people, and you must register by phone by 18:00 on April 9. Participants receive 1 packet of lettuce seeds as a gift, which does not count toward the lending limit.

When choosing seeds, be sure to check the harvest year and germination rate on the packet. If you have questions, ask a volunteer.

Parking space is limited, so please walk or cycle if you can.

Inquiries: Han Seo-yoon, Seed Library coordinator, 010-4471-2286 (weekdays 9:00–17:00). Haetsal Village Residents' Autonomy Committee.
