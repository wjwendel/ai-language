Midorigaoka 3-chome Neighborhood Association (みどりが丘三丁目町内会): Call for participants in the year-end night watch (fire-prevention patrol, "火の用心パトロール"). As every year, this is for fire prevention at the end of the year.

The night-watch patrol runs for five days, from December 26 to December 30. The meeting place is in front of the 3-chome community hall (三丁目集会所). Group 1 gathers at 19:30 and disbands at 20:30; Group 2 gathers at 21:00 and disbands at 22:00. However, on December 30 only, Group 2 will not be held, and the patrol will be done by Group 1 alone.

The neighborhood association will provide wooden clappers (拍子木), flashlights and reflective vests, but only for those who apply by December 20.

Applications must be made by the participants themselves, either by filling in the application form on the circulating notice board (回覧板) or by phoning the office. The exception is elementary school children, whose applications are to be made by a parent or guardian. Elementary school children may join Group 1 only when accompanied by a parent or guardian. Junior high school students may join Group 1 only.

Participants receive a 500-yen shopping voucher for the local shopping street for each patrol they take part in. Those who take part on all five days receive an additional 20% of their total amount. The shopping vouchers will be handed only to the participants themselves, at the community hall after the group disbands on December 30. They cannot be collected by anyone on a participant's behalf.

After the patrol, warm pork miso soup (豚汁) will be served at the community hall. It is free for participants; family members who are not participants pay 150 yen per bowl.

For lost items and for requests to change groups, contact the office: Keiko Tamura (田村恵子), 090-4417-2286 (open 10:00–18:00). To report an absence on the day itself, contact your group leader directly (Group 1: Morikawa (森川); Group 2: Ōno (大野)).

The patrol will take place even on rainy days. It will be cancelled on days when a strong wind advisory has been issued, and this will be announced on the neighborhood association's notice board by 17:00.
