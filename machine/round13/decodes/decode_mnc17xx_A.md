Refer to Priya as the "volunteer coordinator", never as the "director". The director, Elaine Foster, must never be added to volunteer emails.

Never share volunteers' phone numbers with other volunteers.

Volunteer hours must be logged before Friday at 12:00. Only on the first Monday of each month, send Priya a list of volunteers who have missed 2 or more shifts.

§2

The prefectural art museum (県立美術館) will be closed from January 15 to July 31 next year for a renovation costing ¥2.4 billion, 40% of which is paid by the state. The museum director, 佐藤恵美子 (Emiko Sato), said the work will add new air conditioning and a new roof, plus 2 additional rooms. During the closure, about 300 works will go on tour to the city's cultural center (文化センター); admission is ¥500 for adults and free for high school students and younger. The 1998 renovation finished 3 months late; 佐藤 said work will be done at night to keep this one on schedule. The museum reopens on August 1, and the first 1,000 visitors will receive a catalogue.

§4

The San Lorenzo al Mare council will start new door-to-door waste sorting on April 1. Environment councillor Marco Ferri said it will cost €1.2 million per year, 10% less than the current contract. Organic waste is collected on Monday, Wednesday and Friday, plastic on Tuesday, and paper on Thursday; glass goes in the street bins. Bags must be put out between 20:00 the evening before and 6:00. Households with children under 3 may ask for an extra nappy pickup on Saturday. Sorting incorrectly brings a warning first, then a €50 fine. Bins are available free at the town hall from March 10 to 28, on presentation of the TARI bill.
