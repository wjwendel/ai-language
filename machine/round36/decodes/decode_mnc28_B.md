Sunshine Village Seed Library spring lending and return guide: The Sunshine Village (햇살마을) Seed Library, on the first floor of the Sunshine Village Community Center, opens for the first time this spring.

It runs from April 5 to April 26, every Saturday from 10:00 to 13:00, in the front yard of the community center. On rainy days it moves from the front yard to the second-floor auditorium of the community center.

Each person may borrow up to 3 packets of seeds and up to 2 pots of seedlings. You can borrow seeds and seedlings together. The seedlings are tomato, chili pepper and eggplant. Quantities of each variety are limited and given out first come, first served. A volunteer writes each loan in the ledger, so you do not need to fill in a record yourself.

Borrowing seeds is free. Seedling pots carry a deposit of 2,000 won per pot, and half of the deposit is refunded when you return the empty pot. Only residents whose address is in Sunshine Village may borrow, and you must bring an ID that shows your address.

By April 19 you must return all native bean seeds you borrowed, plus any lettuce seeds borrowed before March 10. Return them as seeds you harvested yourself, in the same amount you borrowed. The borrower must return seeds in person, and no one may return them on someone else's behalf. The exception is seniors aged 70 or older, whose family members may return seeds for them. Anyone with unreturned seeds cannot borrow new seeds until they have finished returning them.

To donate or exchange seeds, speak to the information desk during opening hours. Donations must be seeds you collected yourself, not store-bought seeds. Exchanges are limited to 2 packets at a time.

On April 12 at 11:00 there is a free class, '베란다 텃밭 가꾸기' (Balcony Vegetable Gardening), in the second-floor auditorium. It is limited to 20 people. You must sign up by phone by 18:00 on April 9. Each participant gets a gift packet of lettuce seeds, which does not count toward the borrowing limit.

When choosing seeds, be sure to check the harvest year and germination rate on the packet. If you have questions, ask a volunteer. Parking space is tight, so please come on foot or by bicycle if you can.

For inquiries, contact the Seed Library coordinator, 한서윤 (Han Seo-yun), at 010-4471-2286 (weekdays 9:00–17:00). Issued by the Sunshine Village Residents' Autonomy Committee.
