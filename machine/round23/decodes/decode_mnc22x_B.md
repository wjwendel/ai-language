Notice of the 桜台町内会 autumn festival. The festival will be held on October 12 (Saturday) and October 13 (Sunday) at 桜台第二公園. On both days it runs from 10:00 to 20:00, except that on October 13 the 盆踊り runs from 18:30 to 20:30, extended by 30 minutes.

The entry fee is ¥500 for adults and ¥200 for elementary school pupils; preschoolers enter free. For households that have not paid this year's association fee, the fee is ¥800 per adult.

Those applying to run a stall should bring their application form and the stall fee of ¥3,000 to the reception desk of the 町内会館 by September 20.

Help with setup is needed on October 11 (Friday) from 16:00, and help with cleanup on October 14 (Monday, a public holiday) from 9:00.

In case of rain, the festival will be postponed to October 19 (Saturday); this will be announced at 7:00 that morning over the neighborhood broadcast. If it also rains on the 19th, the festival will be cancelled and stall fees will be refunded in full.

There is no parking, so please do not come by car. The exception is wheelchair users, for whom temporary parking with 5 spaces is available on the north side of the park.

Contact: festival committee chair 森田和夫, 090-4417-2286.
