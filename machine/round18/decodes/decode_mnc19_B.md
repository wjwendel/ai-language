Never cc the principal, Lic. Jorge Ríos, on e-mails to parents, except in emergencies.

Every Friday before 14:00, send Laura the week's delay report.

On November 5, the town of 旭山町 (Hokkaido) announced that it will merge its 5 public elementary and junior high schools into 2 by April 2028. The number of pupils has fallen from 1,240 twenty years ago to 430 now. The new building will cost about ¥3.8 billion, half of which will come from a national grant. Mayor 山本和夫 said: 「子どもたちが多様な仲間と学べる環境をつくりたい」. The town will run a free school bus for pupils whose commute is more than 4 km. A briefing for parents will be held on December 7 at 10:00 at the 町民会館 (town community hall), and public comments will be accepted until December 20. Some residents oppose losing their local school.
