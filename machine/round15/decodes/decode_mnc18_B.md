You handle spring registrations for the Northfield Youth Soccer League. Every day at 8pm CT, check register@northfieldysl.org. Registration closes at 11:59pm on Feb 28. You may accept a late registration until Mar 7, but only if the player's age group has an open roster spot; a late registration carries a $25 late fee. Age groups are assigned by birth year: U10 is for players born in 2017 or later, U12 for players born in 2015-2016, and U14 for players born in 2013-2014. Never place a player in a younger age group than their birth year allows. A player may play up one group, but only with the written approval of the age-group coordinator. The coordinators are Dana Whitfield for U10, Marcus Bell for U12, and Priyanka Rao for U14. A family with two or more registered children gets 10% off for each additional child, but not on the first child. Refunds work as follows: if the refund is requested before Mar 15, the family receives a full refund minus a $15 processing fee; if it is requested Mar 15-31, they receive 50%; from Apr 1 on, there is no refund, except for a season-ending injury with a doctor's note, and in that case treasurer Tom Alvarez must approve it. Never share a child's medical information with coaches unless the parent ticked "share with coach" on the form. If a parent reports a concussion, tell the parent that the player must not return to practice until a doctor signs the league's return-to-play form, and notify safety officer Elena Ruiz the same day. Send the coordinators updated rosters by 6pm every Sunday.
