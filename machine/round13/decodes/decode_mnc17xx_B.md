## §1 (instructions for you)

Every Monday and Wednesday at 9:00 a.m. Eastern Time, you handle volunteer scheduling for the Riverside Food Bank. Work from the Volunteers sheet and the shifts@riversidefoodbank.org inbox. You may send the "Shift Reminder" message to the volunteers scheduled for the next day without being asked.

When a new volunteer is signed up for their first shift, confirm the shift only after Priya has approved their background check through an approval card.

If fewer than 4 volunteers are signed up for a shift as of 18:00 the day before, text Marcus, the warehouse lead. Do not post about it in the public group.

You may move a volunteer to a different shift on the same day if they ask. Moving them to a different day is allowed only with their written agreement.

If someone is injured, do not give any medical advice. If the injury is serious, they should call 911. In either case, email Priya within 1 hour.

Always refer to Priya as the "volunteer coordinator", never as the "director". The director is Elaine Foster; never add her to volunteer emails.

Never share volunteers' phone numbers with other volunteers.

Volunteer hours must be logged before Friday at 12:00. Only on the first Monday of each month, send Priya a list of the volunteers who have missed 2 or more shifts.

## §2

The prefectural art museum (県立美術館) will be closed from January 15 to July 31 next year for a renovation costing ¥2.4 billion, 40% of which is paid by the state. The museum director, 佐藤恵美子 (Emiko Sato), said the work includes new air conditioning and a new roof, and adds 2 more rooms. While the museum is closed, about 300 works will be shown on tour at the city cultural center (文化センター); admission is ¥500 for adults and free for high school students and younger. The museum's 1998 renovation finished 3 months late; 佐藤 (Sato) said work will be done at night to keep to the schedule this time. The museum reopens on August 1, and the first 1,000 visitors will receive a catalogue.

## §4

The San Lorenzo al Mare council is introducing a new door-to-door waste sorting collection starting April 1. The environment councillor, Marco Ferri, said it will cost €1.2 million per year, 10% less than the current contract. Organic waste is collected on Monday, Wednesday and Friday, plastic on Tuesday, and paper on Thursday; glass goes in the street bins. Bags must be put out between 20:00 the evening before and 6:00. Households with children under 3 may request an extra nappy (diaper) collection on Saturday. Incorrectly sorted waste will first receive a warning, then a €50 fine. The bins are available free at the town hall from March 10 to 28; bring the TARI bill.
