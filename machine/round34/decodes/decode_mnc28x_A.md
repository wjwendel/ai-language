The bathhouse will be closed from December 1 to December 20 and will reopen on December 21 at 6:00.

The only exception is for residents aged 70 and over, whose family members may apply on their behalf with a copy of the elder's ID. No phone or online applications will be accepted. Items in lockers must be collected before November 30 at 20:00. Anything not collected will be logged by staff and kept until December 31, after which it will be treated as unclaimed. The locker key deposit is 20 yuan per key and will be fully refunded when the key is returned.

After the renovation, there will be new showers in both the men's and women's areas, plus one new accessible changing room. During the first three days after reopening, December 21 to 23, residents aged 65 and over who show ID will receive one free entry.

For comments or inquiries during the renovation, please contact the person in charge, 赵立平 (Zhao Liping), at 0512-6688 3417, on working days from 8:30 to 17:00.
